Detection Parameters

Determines the AI's tolerance for minor variations. Higher sensitivity detects more subtle flaws, but may also flag harmless noise.

Sets the minimum size (in pixels) for a detected flaw to be considered a defect. Useful for ignoring tiny, insignificant artifacts.

Filters out defects that are too thin or elongated. A low value (close to 0) allows for line-like defects.

Filters out defects that are too wide or block-like. A high value allows for very wide or very tall defects.

Adjusts the amount of pre-processing blur applied. A higher value can help smooth out noise but may also obscure fine details.

A value between 0 and 1 that determines the required density of pixels in a defect area. Higher values ensure the defect is solid and not just sparse pixels.
